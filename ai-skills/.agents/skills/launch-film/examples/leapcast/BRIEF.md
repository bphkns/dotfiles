## Intent

A launch video for **Leapcast** in the style of the highly produced motion-graphics product launches posted on X (Linear, Raycast, Arc): music-driven, no voiceover, kinetic typography on a near-black canvas, real product UI shown big and moving (push-ins, parallax, masked reveals, live-typing panels), a few sharp feature beats, one benefit each, and a clean end card. 16:9, 1920×1080, about 55 seconds, 30 fps. Confident, calm, premium. Sell the product (this is a promo, not a site tour).

The one message: **Meetings that keep working after the call.** Leapcast is the meeting room that turns every live conversation into summaries, decisions, action items, searchable knowledge and follow-through, with Ori (the AI assistant) inside the room.

## Assets

- capture/extracted/asset-descriptions.md — the canonical inventory (brand marks, six real product screens, Ori panel, site captures, fonts, SFX).
- capture/extracted/brand-facts.md — verified colors, type, copy lines, features and pricing facts. Only use claims that appear there.
- assets/music/ — the chosen Mixkit track (free license, no attribution).
- assets/sfx/ — bundled SFX library.
- assets/fonts/ — Inter Variable + Schibsted Grotesk Variable.

## Customizations

- No narration and no captions: `music: <mood>` in STORYBOARD.md, no SCRIPT.md. Every line of copy is on-screen typography.
- Beat-aware cuts: the storyboard names the music and the frame cuts should land on musical phrases.
- Show the real screenshots, not rebuilt UI, except where a single moving element is rebuilt on top of the screenshot (e.g. Ori typing an answer, an action item checking itself off, a live caption line appearing).
- End card: wordmark + "Meetings that keep working after the call" + leapcast.ai + "From $5/month · 240 AI minutes included".

## Notes

- Only make claims backed by brand-facts.md. No invented stats, no fake customer logos, no fake testimonials.
- Ori is pink (#FC097F); everything else that is accented is terracotta. Never use a purple/blue "AI gradient".
- Avoid: stock footage, bokeh, browser chrome, cursors wandering, slideshow pacing (front-load then freeze), screensaver motion.
